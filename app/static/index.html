How to save a video

  1. Paste one video link per line, then select Find versions.
  2. Choose Best available or one of the versions shown for that exact video.
  3. Wait for the server progress to reach 100%, then tap the green Download to device button in the queue below.

On iPhone, tap the button once, open Safari’s Downloads (↓), then choose Save to Files.
